Before You Start

A quick word from Ana.

I'm genuinely excited to be doing this with you. The next 12 weeks are broken into three phases, each building on the one before it — nothing about this is random, and nothing is thrown at you all at once. Just show up, do the work in front of you, and trust the process.

📌 Your first step: if you haven't already, complete your Client Intake and Movement Assessment today — those two forms are the foundation everything else is built on.
The Cadence

How this works, week to week.

Once you're up and running, this is the rhythm you'll settle into.

🌿
Daily
Habit Tracker
Two minutes a day. Missing a day is fine — missing a week is a sign we need to talk.
📋
Every Sunday
Weekly Check-In
Submit it Sunday, get feedback back from me. This is how I stay close to your week without needing a call.
💬
Every 2 Weeks
Strategy Session
Our 1-on-1 Zoom call — six total over 12 weeks. Come ready to talk through what's working and what's not.
✨
Weeks 4, 8, 12
Progress + Phase Review
Measurements plus a full reflection on the phase. This is where you'll actually see how far you've come.
